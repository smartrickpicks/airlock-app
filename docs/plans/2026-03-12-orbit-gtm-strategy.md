- Big enough to care about audience insights
- Small enough to not have a data team
- Spend on tools ($50-200/mo typical stack)

- Already using Linktree/Beacons/Stan

## 5-Phase Launch Plan

### Phase 1 — Closed Beta (Week 1-4)

**Goal:** Validate quiz completion + share rates with 50 hand-picked creators.

**Actions:**

1. Recruit 50 creators via DM outreach (mix: 10 micro <10K, 20 mid 10-100K, 15 mid-high 100-500K, 5 macro 500K+)
2. Priority niches: dating/relationships, fitness, personal finance, gaming, productivity — all have strong "type" culture
3. White-glove onboarding: schedule 15-min Brand Spellcast call with each creator
4. Track: quiz completion rate (target >70%), share rate (target >30%), page creation time (<3 min)
5. Collect 10 testimonials minimum

**Outreach template:**

> Hey [Name], I'm building something that tells your fans which "type" of your content they are — think BuzzFeed quiz meets personality science. Your audience takes a 30-sec quiz, gets a shareable result card, and you get real behavioral data about who's watching. Want early access? It's free during beta.

**Success criteria:** >70% quiz completion, >30% share rate, >5 min avg session duration

### Phase 2 — Open Beta (Week 5-8)

**Goal:** Public launch of Creator Tier. PR push on parasocial health angle.

**Actions:**

1. Launch Creator Tier at $19/mo (or free-during-beta)
2. Press angle: "The first creator platform with a conscience"
3. Target publications: The Verge, TechCrunch, Creator Economy newsletter (by Peter Yang), The Publish Press
4. Parasocial health angle for mainstream press: "This app actually tells fans to go outside" — meme-worthy, PR-worthy
5. Enable Cross-Orbit Discovery (fans see recommended creators based on behavioral match)
6. Launch referral program: creators get 1 month free per referral

**Content strategy:**

- Thread: "We built a link-in-bio that uses personality science. Here's why." (founder story)
- Thread: "Your audience has a personality. Here's what that means for your content." (educational)
- Video: Pika B-roll + Optimus Prime gag intro video
- Case study: beta creator who saw highest engagement lift

**Success criteria:** 500 creator signups, 10K fan quiz completions, 3 press mentions

### Phase 3 — OBS Integration (Week 9-12)

**Goal:** Convert streamers from link-in-bio to full platform.

**Actions:**

1. Launch OBS WebSocket v5 Browser Source overlay
2. Live behavioral audience data during streams
3. Partner with 5 streamers for launch day co-streams
4. Clip farming economy (marketplace for clip editors)
5. Target: Twitch, YouTube Live, Kick communities

**Success criteria:** 100 streamers with active OBS overlay, 50 clip marketplace transactions

### Phase 4 — Team Hub (Week 13-16)

**Goal:** Activate retention engine (A-tier).

**Actions:**

1. Team coordination for creator crews
2. Content calendar with behavioral timing insights
3. Shared analytics dashboard
4. Target creator agencies and management companies

**Success criteria:** 20 teams of 3+ members, 60% 30-day team retention

### Phase 5 — Scale (Week 17+)

**Goal:** Network effects compound.

**Actions:**

1. API for third-party integrations
2. Creator-to-creator collaboration tools
3. A/B testing on Orbit Pages
4. International expansion (multi-language personas)
5. Explore token economics for fan engagement rewards

## Risk Mitigation

| Risk                          | Mitigation                                                                    |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Low quiz completion           | A/B test question count (3 vs 5 vs 7), optimize mobile UX                     |
| Privacy backlash              | Identity sovereignty is core — fans control data, can delete anytime          |
| Creator churn                 | Team Hub is the retention engine — harder to leave when team depends on it    |
| Linktree price war            | We don't compete on links — we compete on intelligence. Different value prop. |
| Parasocial health PR backfire | Frame as empowerment, not restriction. Otto's voice, not a therapist's.       |
