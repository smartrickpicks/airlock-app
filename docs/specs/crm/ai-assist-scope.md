# AI Assist Scope

## Purpose

Define where AI adds leverage in the account-memory model without overcommitting to automation.

## High-leverage AI tasks

- classify inbound messages
- summarize long threads
- extract structured facts
- suggest stakeholder links
- detect missing stakeholder roles
- recommend internal owner
- surface action items
- draft follow-up content

## Human-first areas

- final send authority
- ambiguous routing
- weak-confidence stakeholder role assignments
- legal or contractual commitments
- multi-recipient sensitive communication

## Product principle

AI proposes. Humans and explicit rules authorize.

## Demo requirement

The demo should show:

- AI recommendation cards
- confidence language
- blocked or gated next steps
- account-matching suggestions
