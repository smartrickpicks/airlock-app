## Coming Soon

CRM Communications (Smart Line) is a post-MVP feature. It includes:

- **Shared inbox** — Pool model with claim, presence, internal notes, and assignment strategies (manual / round-robin / least-busy / territory)
- **iMessage bridge** — Smart Line routes inbound messages to the shared inbox
- **Web widget** — Embed on your website; visitors initiate iMessage contact from mobile, or submit phone number on desktop
- **AI triage** — Otto qualifies and routes new contacts based on Workflow Engine rules

The prospect-initiated model (visitor sends first text) avoids iMessage rate limit risks entirely.
