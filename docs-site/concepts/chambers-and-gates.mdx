## The Four Chambers

Airlock's contract lifecycle is divided into four chambers. Every vault starts in Discover and progresses to Ship.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Discover" icon="magnifying-glass" color="#EF4444">
    Initial intake and extraction. Otto reads the document and populates all fields. Gate passes when extraction confidence meets threshold.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Build" icon="hammer" color="#F59E0B">
    Human review and enrichment. Builders inspect fields, propose patches, and resolve conflicts. Gate passes when all required fields are verified.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Review" icon="eye" color="#A855F7">
    Gatekeeper approval. The review queue surfaces vaults awaiting sign-off. Gate passes when all approvers approve.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ship" icon="rocket" color="#22C55E">
    Final delivery. Contract is executed, filed, and linked to downstream systems.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Gates

A gate is the checkpoint between two chambers. Gates can:

- **Auto-pass** — when all machine-checkable conditions are met
- **Require human approval** — when a gatekeeper must sign off
- **Fail** — blocking the vault from advancing until resolved
- **Be overridden** — by a Director or Executive org role

## SLA Timers

Every gate has an optional SLA timer. When a vault sits at a gate past its SLA:

1. A signal appears in the vault's activity feed
2. The triage dashboard flags the vault
3. Escalation notifications are sent (if configured)

<Info>
  Gate configuration is per-workspace. Admins define which gates require human approval and what the SLA windows are.
</Info>
