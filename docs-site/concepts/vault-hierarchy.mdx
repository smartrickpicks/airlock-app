## Overview

Every piece of data in Airlock lives inside a vault. Vaults are organized in a four-level tree:

```
Parent (e.g. Acme Records)
  └── Division (e.g. Publishing Arm)
        └── Counterparty (e.g. Summit Publishing)
              └── Item (e.g. Distribution Agreement 2026)
```

This hierarchy is **not a CRM table alongside your data** — it **is** the CRM. The CRM module is simply a different view lens over the same vault tree.

## Levels

| Level | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Parent** | Top-level entity — a company or organization | Acme Records |
| **Division** | A subdivision or business unit | Publishing Arm |
| **Counterparty** | The other party to a contract or deal | Summit Publishing |
| **Item** | A specific contract, deal, or work unit | Distribution Agreement |

## Why the vault IS the CRM

Traditional CLM tools keep contracts and CRM data in separate systems, requiring integrations and sync jobs. In Airlock:

- Contacts, companies, and relationships are modeled as vault nodes
- Contract items are always attached to the counterparty that signed them
- Every interaction (email, call, note) is an event on the vault

<Tip>
  The CRM module browses levels 1–3 of the vault tree. The Contracts module browses level 4 (items).
</Tip>

## Vault States

A vault is always in one of these states:

| State | Description |
|---|---|
| **Active** | In progress, in a chamber |
| **Completed** | Reached Ship gate successfully |
| **Archived** | Removed from active views, not deleted |
| **Blocked** | Stuck at a gate — SLA may be at risk |

Vaults are **never hard-deleted**. Archiving sets a `deleted_at` timestamp (soft delete).
