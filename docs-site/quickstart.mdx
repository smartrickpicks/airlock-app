## Prerequisites

- An Airlock workspace invitation
- A modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Arc, or Safari)

## Step 1: Set up your workspace

When you first log in, the onboarding wizard walks you through:

1. Naming your workspace
2. Inviting your team
3. Assigning org roles (Member, Lead, Director, Executive)

## Step 2: Choose your first module

Airlock opens to the **Triage Dashboard** — your home view. From here you can:

- See all active vaults across modules
- Filter by chamber, assignee, or SLA status
- Drill into any vault to open the Triptych view

## Step 3: Create your first vault

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Contracts">
    Click the Contracts icon in the module bar on the far left.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Triage">
    The triage dashboard shows all active contract vaults.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a contract">
    Drag a PDF into the upload zone. Otto begins extraction immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review extracted fields">
    Open the vault. The Record Inspector shows every extracted field, grouped by section, with confidence scores.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Advance through chambers">
    When the Discover gate passes, the vault moves to Build. Continue through Review → Ship.
  </Step>
</Steps>
