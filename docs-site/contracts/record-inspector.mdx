## Overview

The Record Inspector lives in the Control panel of the Triptych. It displays every field extracted from a contract document, organized into sections, with confidence scores and edit controls.

## Field Cards

Each field is displayed as a card with:

- **Field name** — canonical label from the extraction config
- **Extracted value** — what Otto found in the document
- **Confidence score** — 0–100, color-coded (green / amber / red)
- **Source anchor** — the exact text span that was extracted from
- **Edit control** — propose a patch directly from the card

## Sections

Fields are grouped into collapsible sections. Tier 1 sections (core deal terms) are expanded by default. Tier 2+ sections are collapsed.

Example sections for a Distribution Agreement:
- Deal Terms (Tier 1)
- Territory & Rights (Tier 1)
- Royalties & Advances (Tier 1)
- Delivery Requirements (Tier 2)
- Special Provisions (Tier 2)
- Execution (Tier 2)

## Confidence Thresholds

| Score | Color | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 80–100 | Green | High confidence — auto-accepted at Discover gate |
| 50–79 | Amber | Medium confidence — flagged for human review |
| 0–49 | Red | Low confidence — must be manually verified before gate |

## Entity Resolution

When Otto extracts a company or person name that could match multiple records in the vault hierarchy, the field card shows an inline disambiguation UI — not a modal, not a CRM cross-link.
